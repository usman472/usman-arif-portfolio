About

Understanding the business before writing any code

I'm an AI consulting and automation practitioner based in Qatar, building a practice around a simple belief: automation only creates value when it solves a real operational problem. That means the work starts with understanding how a business actually runs — not with a tool.

My interest is practical AI. Not demos, not experiments that stay in a notebook — systems that remove repetitive work, make sure nothing gets dropped, and give people back their attention for the parts of the job that genuinely need a human.

I'm at the beginning of building this practice. I'm being direct about that: there's no client roster to show you yet. What I bring right now is the ability to build, and the willingness to sit with a business owner and understand their process properly before proposing anything.

Industry Focus

Qatar Real Estate

My initial area of focus. Property businesses handle high enquiry volume with small teams — which makes the gaps between enquiry and response the most expensive part of the operation.

Property Enquiries

Enquiries arrive through portals, WhatsApp, calls, social media and walk-ins. When they land in different places, some simply never get answered.

Lead Management

Without a structured record of who enquired, about what, and when they were last contacted, follow-up depends entirely on memory.

Customer Communication

Slow or inconsistent first responses cost deals. Buyers and tenants typically move on to whoever replies first.

Follow-up Workflows

Most property enquiries are not ready immediately. Without a systematic follow-up process, warm leads quietly go cold.

If you run a property business in Qatar and slow follow-up is costing you leads, I'd like to understand how your process works today. The useful starting point is the problem, not a pitch.
